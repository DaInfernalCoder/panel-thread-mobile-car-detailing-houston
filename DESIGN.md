# Panel Thread design direction

## Subject, audience, and job

Mobile detailing for Houston drivers who want consistent attention carried across cabin, glass, wheels, and exterior panels. The page should make that continuity visible and lead to calling or booking online once contact details are confirmed.

## Visual system

- Panel ink — `#172329`
- Seam slate — `#294047`
- Workshop sage — `#E3E8E2`
- Finish white — `#FAFCF8`
- Thread teal — `#5EA6A0`
- Display: Archivo Black
- Body: Manrope
- Utility: IBM Plex Mono

## Layout thesis

The editorial frame uses a stitched service line to connect each surface rather than treating services as isolated cards.

## Signature

One dashed thread crosses the P mark and continues through the service sequence.

## Critique

The first concept felt like upholstery branding. The revision keeps the thread geometric and ties it to panel-to-panel inspection.
